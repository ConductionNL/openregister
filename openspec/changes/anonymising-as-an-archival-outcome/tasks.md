# Tasks: anonymising-as-an-archival-outcome

## 1. The fourth action

- [x] 1.1 `anonymiseren` joins the archival action vocabulary and its validator. (verified: lib/Service/Archival/Appraisal.php, lib/Service/Archival/ArchivalDeclarationReader.php, tests/Unit/Service/Archival/AnonymisationTest.php, tests/Unit/Service/Archival/ArchivalDeclarationReaderActionTest.php)
- [x] 1.2 The nomination deriver can produce it, and a destruction list can carry it. (built: ArchivalNominationService already passes a selectielijst row's or an annotation's `anonymiseren` through as the record's archiefnominatie; ArchivalNominationServiceTest now pins both. RetentionService::findEligibleForAnonymisation / anonymisationRefusalIn apply the destruction rules (live, due, not frozen, not held, not listed, not held back by an ancestor) to an anonymise nomination, and DestructionCheckJob builds one list over both sets; every list entry carries `action` (`destroy` or `anonymise`, RetentionService::listActionOf). An approval never destroys an `anonymise` entry unless its reviewer answered `destroy`: DestructionService::isWithheldFromDestruction takes it off at approval (`uitgezonderd`, reason says it is nominated for anonymising) and DestructionListRun refuses it at execution, which now also refuses a recorded `anonymise` answer. Carrying out the anonymisation of withheld entries is the sweep job, not built here.)
- [x] 1.3 A reviewer may answer an entry with it, recorded like the other three answers. (verified: lib/Service/Archival/DestructionReviewServiceInterface.php `ANSWER_ANONYMISE`, lib/Service/Archival/ReviewOutcomeService.php, lib/Service/Archival/DestructionService.php, tests/Unit/Service/Archival/DestructionReviewServiceTest.php, ReviewOutcomeServiceTest.php, DestructionWithholdingTest.php)
  - `anonymise` is the fourth review answer: it lands in the list's one decision history with reviewer, moment and reason, changes nothing on the record at answer time (like `destroy`), hands nothing to a transfer list, and withholds the entry from destruction (`uitgezonderd`, the reviewer's reason quoted), so an approved list never destroys a record somebody said to anonymise. Carrying the anonymisation out for such an entry is the wiring of 1.2/3.2, not done here.

## 3. The act

- [x] 3.1 Execution runs through the existing pseudonymise primitive, per the declared profile. (verified: lib/Service/Archival/AnonymisationService.php, tests/Unit/Service/Archival/AnonymisationTest.php)
- [x] 3.2 The search index, the history projection and every derived copy are updated in the same act. (built: lib/Service/Archival/AnonymisationDerivedCopies.php lists the derived copies of a record as AnonymisationTargets, so AnonymisationRun prepares all before writing any: StateHistoryAnonymisationTarget drops the record's intervals of the changed properties, open and closed (new StateHistoryMapper::countForObjectProperties/deleteForObjectProperties); ContentIndexAnonymisationTarget drops its content-search chunks (source_type object; `deleteBySource` added to ChunkStoreInterface, which ChunkMapper already had); VectorAnonymisationTarget drops its semantic-search vectors under the uuid and the database id. Chunks and vectors are cut again from the anonymised record by the next extraction/vectorisation. Property search reads the record's own row, so it follows the record write. Not yet done: the target that writes the record itself, and the job that runs a sweep with these targets (with 1.2).)
- [x] 3.3 The anonymised values are removed from the audit trail's stored diffs, an entry records the act, and the chain is re-sealed. (built: lib/Service/Archival/AuditTrailAnonymisationTarget.php is the trail as an AnonymisationTarget. prepare() reads the record's stored diffs (new lib/Db/AuditTrail/AuditDiffStore.php, tombstones left out), redacts them in memory with AuditDiffRedactionTarget, refuses when any diff of the record would still hold a removed value (current or former, text of 3+ characters, not one the anonymised record itself keeps; the refusal counts and never quotes), and verifies the chain from the first row it will rewrite. apply() rewrites the diffs, inserts an `archival.anonymise` entry naming the profile, the changed and kept properties, the decision and actor (AnonymisationAuthority), the rewritten row ids and the pre-rewrite chain verdict, and re-seals from the first rewritten row (new AuditHashService::resealFrom via lib/Service/Audit/AuditResealInterface.php; not fail-soft: an unavailable seal lock leaves the act in progress))
- [x] 3.4 A record under a legal hold is not anonymised, and the refusal names the hold. (verified: lib/Service/Archival/AnonymisationRun.php, tests/Unit/Service/Archival/AnonymisationRunTest.php)
- [x] 3.5 A failure to reach a derived copy fails the whole act; nothing is half-anonymised. (verified: lib/Service/Archival/AnonymisationRun.php, tests/Unit/Service/Archival/AnonymisationRunTest.php)
