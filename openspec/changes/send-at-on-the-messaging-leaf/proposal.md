# Proposal: send-at-on-the-messaging-leaf

## Summary

Let a message be sent at a chosen moment. The dispatch leaf
(`messaging-dispatch-leaf`) sends one caller-composed body to one named
OpenConnector source, now. This change adds `sendAt` to the send endpoints,
a queue of scheduled messages bound to an object, a list and a cancel per
object, and a bounded sweep that dispatches what is due. It also adds
e-mail as a third channel of the leaf over a seeded SMTP source, because the
ledger row is about e-mail and the leaf had only SMS and WhatsApp.

## What changes

- `POST /api/integrations/{channel}/send` accepts an optional `sendAt`
  (ISO 8601, in the future) and an optional `object` (register, schema,
  id). With `sendAt` the message is stored as `scheduled` and the response
  is 202 with its id; without it the leaf sends now as today.
- `GET /api/objects/{register}/{schema}/{id}/scheduled-messages` lists the
  object's scheduled and sent messages for a user with `read`;
  `DELETE /api/scheduled-messages/{id}` cancels a scheduled one for the
  author or a user with `update` on the object.
- A `ScheduledMessageSweepJob` dispatches due messages in bounded batches
  through `MessageDispatchProvider`, records the provider response, and
  marks each `sent` or `failed` with the cause. A failure is retried on the
  provider's degrade contract three times and then left `failed`.
- An `email` channel: `POST /api/integrations/email/send` over the seeded
  source `smtp-mail`, body `{to, subject, text, html?, replyTo?}`, dispatched
  through the same router. The message's RFC `Message-ID` is generated by
  the leaf and returned (`reply-threading-by-headers` records it on the
  object).
- Scheduling, sending, cancelling and failing write an audit entry on the
  bound object.

## Consumers

- dossiq: a Send later option on the case mail dialog. Specified in dossiq
  by the dossiq lane (register row 6.9).
- pipelinq (planned follow-ups), humaniq (scheduled HR letters), portaliq
  (reminders to a citizen).

## ADRs

- ADR-067 (shared egress plane): every send leaves through an OpenConnector
  source.
- ADR-091: the mailbox that receives is integriq's; the leaf only sends.
- ADR-069: a `TimedJob` in `lib/BackgroundJob/`.
- ADR-022, ADR-078.
