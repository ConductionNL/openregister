## Why

Voorzitter and main contact, "Alle 2 contactpersonen". The second one has to come from the cast, because a fragment
never types a name. Ruben decided (24 Oct) that a contact person may have no BSN, only a name and an address, so the
second contact person is the example of exactly that.
