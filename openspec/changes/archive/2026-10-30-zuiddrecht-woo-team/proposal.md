## Why

The dossiq boards name a team, Woo en informatie. Pieter Jansen works in it, Anouk Bakker leads it, and it is the team on the Lindelaan Woo case and in the share dialog of a saved view. The Zuiddrecht cast had no such group, and it listed Anouk as a legal adviser in Juridische zaken.

## What Changes

- The cast gains the group `woo-en-informatie` (`zuiddrecht-woo-en-informatie`, "Woo en informatie").
- Pieter Jansen and Thomas Kuiper join that group.
- Anouk Bakker becomes the team leader of Woo en informatie.
