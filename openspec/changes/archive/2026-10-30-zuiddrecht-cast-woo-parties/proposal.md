## Why

- J. van Wijk, the authorised representative of Aannemersbedrijf Van Wijk B.V.;
- Bewonersvereniging Lindelaan, a reporter without an account.

- New cast person J. van Wijk (`jeroen.vanwijk`), with a valid example BSN and a BRP entry. He becomes Van Wijk's contact.
- New cast organisation Bewonersvereniging Lindelaan (`bewonerslindelaan`, a Vereniging, example KvK number 00001006), with KvK and establishment entries.
