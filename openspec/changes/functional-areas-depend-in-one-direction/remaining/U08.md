The intake-source reference is not removed by re-rowing the slugs interface to Core. That re-row lowers the Platform to Processes line count from 31 to 30, and the transitive rule of the violation degree then counts that downward line. The degree would rise above the slack, so the Core contract is used instead.

The elevation attribution was kept in wave 3 (#10822): a mapper built without its container would stop stamping the elevation. It is now asked through the container, as the other ambient frames the mapper reads already are. In the app, the container always answers, because `FlowWiring` binds the port. A mapper whose container answers nothing stamps no elevation, and `ObjectActionElevationTest` pins both cases.
