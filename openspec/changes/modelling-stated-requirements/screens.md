# Screens

- `openregister/OrSchema`: the schema page with its property list and the add property action (mod-visual-builder).
- `openregister/OrEigenschap`: the property editor, including the concept scheme field (mod-visual-builder, mod-vocabulary, mod-json-field-schema).
- `openregister/OrObjectBewerken`: the record form that shows the refusal next to the field (mod-validate).
